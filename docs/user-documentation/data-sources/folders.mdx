---
title: "Folders"
description: "Upload documents and tables to a folder in a Space, or send them with the API, to give agents knowledge that isn't in a connected tool."
---

A folder holds documents and tables that you add to Dust yourself. Use folders for content that isn't in a connected tool, like exported reports, CSV files, or documents generated by your own systems. Agents use folder content like any other data source: they can search its documents and query its tables.

## Create a folder

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Space">
    Open the Space where the content should be available, and click **Add data** > **Upload data**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the folder">
    Click **Create folder**, give it a name and a description, and save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only people with access to the Space can use the folder in their agents.

## Add content

In the folder, click **Add data** and pick an option:

- **Create a document**: paste text or upload a file, like a PDF, a Word document, or a text file. The size limit depends on your plan and is shown in the form.
- **Create a table**: upload a CSV file. Agents query tables with [table queries](/docs/user-documentation/agents/knowledge/table-queries).
- **Upload files**: add several documents at once.

To keep a folder in sync with another system, send documents and tables with the API. See [Upsert a document](/api-reference/datasources/upsert-a-document-in-a-data-source) and [Upsert a table](/api-reference/datasources/upsert-a-table).

## Folders and @dust

The @dust agent searches the folders in the Company Data Space, along with your connections. Folders in other Spaces are only used by the agents they're added to.
