## Plans

Dust offers two plans:

- **Business**: available with a free option (no credit card required, up to 5 users, 3 connector, 5 Spaces) or with paid Pro and Max seats for more capacity (up to 100 seats, up to 3 connectors, 5 Spaces).
- **Enterprise**: unlimited users and connectors, unlimited Spaces, SSO, SCIM, audit logs, and advanced credit controls. Contact sales to get started.

Pro and Max seats come with a monthly credit allocation. The Free seat is meant for discovering Dust: it comes with a one-time allocation of 500 credits, and a member can only have it once. After a member moves from a Free seat to a Pro or Max seat, they can't go back to a Free seat. Learn more in [Credits](/docs/user-documentation/admins/usage-seats-and-credits/credits) and [Seat management](/docs/user-documentation/admins/usage-seats-and-credits/seat-management).

## Subscribing to the Business plan

### How do I subscribe to the Business plan?

You can subscribe in two ways:

- **At sign-up**: when creating your workspace, select a Pro or Max seat to go directly to checkout.
- **From the free option**: start with a free workspace and upgrade any member's seat to Pro or Max from **Admin > Credits**, **Members** tab. This triggers the checkout flow.

At checkout, you are billed for one full billing period (monthly or annual) for the paid seat.

### Why do I need to verify a phone number?

When you create a new workspace, Dust asks you to verify a phone number with a code sent by text message.

- Use a mobile number. Other numbers, such as landlines or prepaid numbers, aren't accepted.
- A phone number can only verify one Dust workspace. If Dust says the number is already used by another workspace, use a different mobile number, or contact [support@dust.tt](mailto:support@dust.tt).
- You can request a new code up to 3 times per number every 30 minutes, and try up to 3 different numbers per workspace per day.

### How do I pay as a business?

To pay as a company on Dust with Stripe:

1. At checkout, choose "I'm purchasing as a business".
2. Enter your company name and VAT number correctly.
3. Click 'Save' to save your payment method, then confirm the payment to complete the purchase.

A Stripe invoice is generated and the new seat is available immediately.

## How Business plan billing works

Your billing period is anchored to your subscription start date and renews on that same date each month or year. Billing is per seat: seats can be billed **monthly** or **annually**, and both billing cycles can coexist in the same workspace.

- **Monthly seats**: billed upfront each month based on seats active at the start of the period.
- **Annual seats**: billed at a lower monthly rate, for the full year.

### Adding seats mid-period

Mid-period seat additions are prorated from the date the seat is added through the end of the current subscription term, and invoiced in the next billing month.

Here is an example:

Let's assume you have 2 Pro seats at the start of your pay period (September 3rd – October 2nd) and add a third seat on September 18th.

- On September 3rd, you are charged upfront for 2 seats for the September period.
- On October 3rd, you are charged upfront for 3 seats for October, **plus** a prorated charge for the 15 days of the added September seat.

### Removing seats

- **Monthly seats** removed mid-period are credited on a prorated basis to the next invoice.
- **Annual seats** are returned to your seat stock when unassigned. They can be reassigned to another member at the end of the current credit period. Seats vacated when someone leaves are not refunded.

### Changing a seat type

Upgrading a member from Pro to Max takes effect immediately, and the Max seat is billed pro rata for the rest of the period on your next invoice. Downgrading from Max to Pro is deferred, and the member keeps Max access until then. See [Changing an existing member's seat](/docs/user-documentation/admins/usage-seats-and-credits/seat-management#changing-an-existing-member%E2%80%99s-seat) for when each change takes effect.

## Managing your subscription

You can manage your subscription from **Admin > Billing** on credit-priced plans, or **Admin > Subscription** on non-credit-priced plans. From there, you can upgrade your plan, update your payment details, and access invoices.

## Invoices and billing details

### How do I change my billing details or who receives invoices?

Invoices are sent to the billing email. To change it, or to update the billing name, phone, or address:

1. Go to **Admin > Billing** and click **Change** next to **Billing contact**. On non-credit-priced plans, go to **Admin > Subscription** and click **Your billing dashboard on Stripe**.
2. The Stripe billing portal opens in a new tab. Update your details there.

Changes apply to future invoices.

### Can I correct an invoice that was already issued?

No. Once an invoice is finalized, it can't be edited. Update your billing details as described above, and the new details will appear on your next invoices.

### How do I get an invoice as a PDF?

Invoices are emailed as a link, not as a PDF attachment. Open the link to view the invoice and download it as a PDF.

You can also find your invoices in **Admin > Billing**, under **Past invoices**: click **See invoice** to open one, or **See all** to see your full invoice history in the Stripe billing portal.

### Can I get a signed contract?

Not on the Business plan. When you subscribe, you accept the [Master Services Agreement](https://dust-tt.notion.site/Master-Services-Agreement-2bdcf30156db4a40bcb20d27b0b1bd4e) and the [Data Processing Addendum](https://dust-tt.notion.site/Data-Processing-Addendum-466528e861e34f08949428e06eecd5f4) at checkout. These terms apply to your subscription.

## What happens when you cancel your subscription?

To cancel, go to **Admin > Billing** on a credit-priced plan or **Admin > Subscription** on a non-credit-priced plan, then click **Cancel subscription**. Your subscription stays active until the end of the current billing period. Until then, you can click **Resume subscription** on the same page to keep your plan.

When the billing period ends, your workspace doesn't switch back to the free option. Instead:

- Nobody can use the workspace anymore. Admins see a page asking them to choose a paid plan to continue. Other members can't access the workspace until an admin subscribes again.
- Connections stop syncing, and triggers stop running.
- Admins receive an email saying when the workspace data will be deleted, and a reminder 3 days before.
- By default, 30 days after the end of the billing period, Dust deletes the workspace data: conversations, agents, skills, data sources and connections, Spaces, and API keys. SSO and SCIM are turned off.

Your original data in the tools you connected (Notion, Slack, Google Drive, etc.) isn't affected. Dust cannot and will not alter the original data.

### What if we want to reactivate our subscription?

An admin can choose a plan from the page shown when they open the workspace. If you subscribe again before your data is deleted, connections and triggers start again, and your conversations, agents, and data are still there.

### What if we want to delete everything?

Send us an email at [support@dust.tt](mailto:support@dust.tt). We will delete your account and all associated data, except for data we are legally required to keep (such as invoicing data). Note that this action is irreversible: all custom agents, data sources, documents, and conversations will be permanently deleted.
