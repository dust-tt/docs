title: "Users and groups provisioning"
---

Dust supports automated user and group management through SCIM 2.0 (System for Cross-domain Identity Management). With this enterprise feature, users are created, updated, and deactivated in Dust automatically from your IdP.

SCIM provisioning can be enabled for enterprise users. You'll need administrative access to both Dust and your IdP.

1. In Dust, go to **Admin > Members**, open the **Groups** tab, click **User provisioning**, then **Set up Directory sync**
2. Select your identity provider (IdP) from the list
3. Follow the steps
4. **Important note for Enterprise customers:** to enable department-segmented analytics, customers must provision relevant groups to Dust.

<Info>
  Check your IdP documentation along the way The setup experience may vary depending on the selected IdP. We recommend checking the documentation of your IdP (specifically the sections about SSO, SAML and SCIM) as each might have specificities. (_For example, [Okta (OIDC)](https://help.okta.com/en-us/content/topics/apps/apps_app_integration_wizard_scim.htm) might require you to create 2 applications - one for SSO, one for SCIM_)
</Info>

## How groups are synced

Dust syncs the groups your IdP sends as SCIM groups, with their members. It doesn't read group membership from user attributes. If your IdP only sends group names as a user attribute, users are provisioned but aren't added to any group, and Dust shows no error.

- **Okta**: in your SCIM app, use **Push Groups** to send each group you need in Dust.
- **Other IdPs**: check how your IdP sends groups over SCIM. Usually, you assign the groups (not only the users) to the SCIM app.

If a user is missing from a group in Dust, check that the group is pushed to Dust and that the user is a member of it in your IdP. Then push the group again.

## Assign roles from groups

You can automatically grant the **Admin** or **Manager** role to your users based on their group membership. In Dust, go to **Admin > Members**, **Roles** tab (admin only) and map any workspace group to a role:

- Members of a group mapped to a role inherit that role.
- A user always keeps the highest role granted across their groups (admin \> manager).
- A group can grant at most one role.

Any workspace group can be mapped to a role — it does not need to be provisioned from your IdP, so manually managed groups work too. For provisioned groups, create the group in your IdP, assign the appropriate members, and synchronize it, then map it to a role in Dust.

<Info>
  This replaces the previous behavior where roles were assigned through groups named exactly "**dust-admins**" and "**dust-managers**".
</Info>
