As a workspace admin, you decide who uses Dust, which data and tools their agents can reach, and how your team gets started. Follow these steps in order to get your workspace ready.

<Tip>
To find a setting quickly, press **⌘K** (Mac) or **Ctrl+K** (Windows and Linux) and pick the **Settings** category, or click **Search** at the top of the admin sidebar. Type what you're looking for, such as "Slack workflows", to jump straight to the right page. See [Search with Command K](/docs/user-documentation/getting-started/intro-to-dust#search-with-command-k).
</Tip>

## 1. Invite your team

Go to **Admin** > **Members** and invite the people who will use Dust. Give each person one of three roles: **Admin**, **Manager**, or **Member**. Managers can manage members, groups and roles, and see workspace analytics, which helps when several teams roll out Dust. See [Workspace governance](/docs/user-documentation/admins/admin-governance/workspace-governance-roles-groups-and-permissions) for what each role can do.

To let people sign in with your identity provider and keep members in sync automatically, set up [Single Sign-On](/docs/user-documentation/admins/admin-governance/single-sign-on-sso/single-sign-on-sso) and [user provisioning](/docs/user-documentation/admins/admin-governance/users-and-groups-provisioning) on plans that include them.

## 2. Connect your company data

Agents give better answers when they can read your company's knowledge. You can bring in three kinds of data:

- **Connections**: tools like Slack, Notion, Google Drive, or Confluence. They sync automatically, so agents always see recent information.
- **Websites**: public websites that Dust crawls on the schedule you choose.
- **Folders**: files you upload yourself, like a folder on your computer.

To add your first connection:

1. Go to **Spaces** > **Connections**, click **Add connections**, and pick a tool.
2. Click **Connect** (**Acknowledge and connect** for Google Drive) and sign in to your account.
3. Select which channels, folders, or pages Dust can sync.

See [Connections](/docs/user-documentation/data-sources/connections) for setup details on each tool.

<Warning>
Synced data isn't available to anyone yet. To let people and agents use it, add it to a Space (next step).
</Warning>

## 3. Decide who sees what with Spaces

Spaces control which people and agents can use which data and tools.

- **Company Data** is the default Space. Every member can use the data in it, and so can **@dust**.
- **Open spaces** are available to all members.
- **Restricted spaces** are limited to the people and groups you add, for example HR or Finance.

Start by adding the data everyone needs to Company Data, then create restricted Spaces for sensitive data. See [Spaces management](/docs/user-documentation/admins/spaces-management).

## 4. Turn on tools

Tools let agents act in other apps, like creating a deal in HubSpot or posting in Slack. Some tools, like web search, are ready to use. Others need an admin to add them first:

1. Go to **Spaces** > **Tools**, click **Add tools**, and pick a tool.
2. Choose whether each person connects their own account or everyone shares one account. See [Personal vs. shared credentials](/docs/user-documentation/admins/tools-management/personal-vs-shared-credentials).

See [Tools](/docs/user-documentation/agents/tools/index) for the full list and each tool's setup steps.

## 5. Bring Dust into Slack

Your team can call agents from Slack: mention **@Dust** in a channel or thread, or send the app a direct message. The agent can use the Slack conversation as context, and everyone in the channel sees the answer.

1. Go to **Admin** > **Integrations** and turn on **Slack bot**.
2. Follow the Slack authorization flow to install the Dust app in your Slack workspace.
3. Add the Dust app to the Slack channels where your team wants to use it.

The Slack bot is separate from the [Slack connection](/docs/user-documentation/admins/connections-management/slack), which syncs Slack messages into Dust as company data. You can set up one without the other.

To let a Slack workflow call an agent, for example on a schedule or when someone adds an emoji reaction, you must first allow the workflow in Dust. See [Slack workflows](/docs/user-documentation/agents/integrations/dust-in-slack/slack-workflows). For everything else, see [Dust in Slack](/docs/user-documentation/agents/integrations/dust-in-slack/index).

<Info>
Exporting **@Dust** conversations from Slack works like exporting direct messages: only Owners and admins of a Slack Business+ or Enterprise Grid account can do it.
</Info>

## 6. Know what @dust can answer

[**@dust**](/docs/user-documentation/agents/default-agents/dust) is the general-purpose agent in every workspace. It answers questions from your company data, and includes skills like [Go Deep](/docs/user-documentation/agents/go-deep) and [Discover Knowledge](/docs/user-documentation/agents/discover-knowledge) by default.

What **@dust** can access:

- **@dust** uses the data in **Company Data**, the default Space of your workspace. To make data available to **@dust**, add it to Company Data.
- **@dust** doesn't search other [Spaces](/docs/user-documentation/admins/spaces-management) on its own, whether they're open or restricted. To use that data:
  - **In one conversation:** type `/` in the message bar, choose **Attach**, and pick a document or folder from any Space you have access to. If it comes from a restricted Space, only members of that Space can access the conversation.
  - **For repeated use:** build an agent that has that data as [knowledge](/docs/user-documentation/agents/knowledge/index). Only members of the Spaces the agent uses can see and use it.
- In a Pod, **@dust** also uses the Pod's [files and linked data](/docs/user-documentation/pods/files).
- There's no setting to choose which data sources **@dust** uses. Admins can only enable or disable it.

## 7. Feature agents and skills for your team

Help people find the right agents and skills from day one. Featured items appear as shortcuts under the message bar on the homepage, and in **Discover**.

1. On the homepage, open **Discover** and stay on the **Featured** tab.
2. Next to **Curated by** your workspace, click **Manage**.
3. Choose **Everyone** or a group, add up to 3 agents or skills, and click **Save**.

See [Feature agents and skills on the homepage](/docs/user-documentation/admins/agents-management#feature-agents-and-skills-on-the-homepage).

## 8. Keep an eye on usage

- **Admin** > **Credits** shows your workspace's credits and how they're used. See [Credits](/docs/user-documentation/admins/usage-seats-and-credits/credits).
- **Admin** > **Analytics** shows how your team uses Dust.
