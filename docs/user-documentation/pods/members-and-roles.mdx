---
title: "Members and roles"
---


Members are managed from the **members panel** in the Pod header. To open it, click the member avatars in the top-right of the Pod header, or click the gear icon (**Pod settings**) and select **Manage members**. Every Pod member can open the panel to see who is in the Pod. Only Editors can add, promote, or remove members.

## Pod roles

| Role       | What they can do                                                                                                                            |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Member** | Read conversations, start new conversations, create and work on tasks, upload files                                                         |
| **Editor** | Everything Members can do, plus: add/remove members, change roles, rename the Pod, edit the description, toggle visibility, archive the Pod |

<Warning>
**A Pod must always have at least one Editor. The role and remove actions are not available for the last remaining Editor. An Editor can leave the Pod only if at least one other Editor exists.**
</Warning>

## How to add members

**From the members panel:**

1. Open the members panel
2. Under **Add people**, search for people by name or email, or for groups by name
3. Click a person or group in the results to add them to the Pod

They are added right away as Members. To make someone an Editor, change their role as described below.

Added members receive a notification.


**By mentioning someone in a conversation:**

If you `@mention` someone who is not yet a member of the Pod, Dust will prompt you to add them as a member. This works if the Pod is Open or if you are an Editor of the Pod.

## How to change a member's role

1. Open the members panel
2. Under **Members of the Pod**, hover over the member or group
3. Click **Set as editor** or **Remove from editors**

The change applies right away, with no confirmation.


## How to remove a member

1. Open the members panel
2. Under **Members of the Pod**, hover over the member or group
3. Click the remove button (**Remove access**)

The removal applies right away, with no confirmation. You can't remove yourself from the panel. To leave the Pod, follow the steps below.

## How to leave a Pod

1. Open the Pod
2. Click the gear icon (**Pod settings**) in the top-right of the Pod header
3. Select **Leave the Pod**

If you are the last Editor, the option is disabled. Add another Editor before leaving.
