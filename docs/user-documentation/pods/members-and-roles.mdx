---
title: "Members and roles"
description: "Add people to a Pod, change their role, remove them, leave a Pod, or archive it."
---

Each person in a Pod is a Member or an Editor. Everyone in the Pod can see who its members are. Only Editors can add, promote, or remove members.

To see the members of a Pod, click the member avatars at the top right of the Pod. You can also click the gear icon (**Pod settings**) and select **Manage members**.

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Member** | Read and start conversations, create and work on tasks, and upload files. |
| **Editor** | Everything a Member can do, and manage members, visibility, and the Pod settings. |

The person who creates the Pod is its first Editor.

## Add members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the members panel">
    Click the member avatars at the top right of the Pod.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite people or groups">
    In **Invite people or groups**, search for the people or groups to add, then add them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

In an Open Pod, people in the workspace can also join by themselves.

## Change a member's role

In the members panel, open the menu next to the person and select **Member** or **Editor**.

## Remove a member

In the members panel, open the menu next to the person and select **Remove access**.

## Change the visibility

In the members panel, Editors can choose who can access the Pod:

- **Only members of the Pod:** the Pod is Restricted.
- **Everyone in the workspace:** the Pod is Open.

Dust asks you to confirm before it changes the visibility.

## Leave a Pod

Click the gear icon (**Pod settings**) and select **Leave the Pod**. You can't leave a Pod that you created, or a Pod where you're the last Editor.

## Archive a Pod

Editors can archive a Pod when its work is done:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Pod settings">
    Click the gear icon (**Pod settings**) and select **More settings…**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Archive the Pod">
    In the **General** tab, under **Archive and delete**, archive the Pod.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An archived Pod no longer shows in your sidebar. Its content stays, and agents can still use it as a data source. To restore the Pod, click **Unarchive** in the same place.

Editors can also delete a Pod from the same section. Deleting a Pod can't be undone.
