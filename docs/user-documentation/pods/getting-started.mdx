This page shows you how to create a Pod, invite your teammates, and start your first conversation in it. For what Pods are, see the [Overview](/docs/user-documentation/pods/overview).

## Create a Pod

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Pod creation dialog">
    In the left sidebar, click **New Pod**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the Pod">
    In **Pod name**, enter a name that tells your team what the Pod is about, like a project, a customer, or a team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the visibility">
    New Pods are Restricted: only invited members can access them. To let anyone in the workspace find and join the Pod, turn on the access switch to make it Open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the Pod">
    Click **Create**. You're the first Editor of the Pod.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the access switch is turned off with "Open Pods are disabled by your workspace admin.", your workspace admins allow only Restricted Pods. See [Admin controls](/docs/user-documentation/pods/admin-controls).

## Invite your team

Click the member avatars at the top right of the Pod, then add people or groups in **Invite people or groups**. See [Members and roles](/docs/user-documentation/pods/members-and-roles).

## Start working in the Pod

A Pod has four tabs:

- **Home:** the Pod's overview.
- **Conversations:** the conversations of the Pod. Mention an agent to start one. See [Conversations](/docs/user-documentation/pods/conversations).
- **Tasks:** the Pod's work items. See [Tasks](/docs/user-documentation/pods/tasks).
- **Files:** the Pod's files and knowledge. See [Files](/docs/user-documentation/pods/files).

Add the files that your team and agents need in the **Files** tab first. Agents in the Pod use them as context.
