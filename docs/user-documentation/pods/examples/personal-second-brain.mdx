---
title: "Personal second brain"
description: "Use a Pod that only you can access as a private workspace for your notes, tasks, and daily briefs."
---

Anyone can use a Restricted Pod with themselves as the only member as a private workspace. It keeps your notes and documents, tracks your commitments as tasks, and gives agents the context they need, so you don't have to explain it again in each conversation.

## Set up the Pod

| Setting | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Your name, or a name like "My workspace". |
| Access | **Restricted**, with you as the only member. |
| Files | Your notes, documents, and reference material. Link the Company Data you use most with **Add** > **From Company Data**. |
| Instructions for agents | Your role, current priorities, and how you like to work. Update them when your priorities change. See [Give agents instructions for the Pod](/docs/user-documentation/pods/files#give-agents-instructions-for-the-pod). |

## Start simple

Use the Pod as a notebook and a task list. Start conversations to think through a problem, draft a document, or ask questions about your notes. Add tasks as commitments come up, and click the play button (**Start working on task**) to hand one to an agent.

## Add a daily brief

Add a [schedule](/docs/user-documentation/agents/triggers/schedules) to an agent, for example every weekday at 8 AM, and pick your Pod under **Where to create this conversation?**. Each morning, the agent posts a brief in the Pod with:

- Tasks that are due or overdue.
- Today's meetings.
- Messages that need your attention, like emails, Slack mentions, or review requests.
- One suggested focus for the day.

The agent needs a tool for each source, like Google Calendar, Gmail, Slack, or GitHub. See [Discover tools](/docs/user-documentation/agents/discover-tools).

## Go further

With your main tools connected, set up agents that:

- Turn requests from your emails and Slack messages into tasks in the Pod, a few times a day.
- Run a weekly review that reads the Pod's activity and suggests what to carry forward, close, or hand off.
- Draft a summary of what you got done each Friday, ready to share with your manager or team.

## What you get

Your Pod knows your context, tracks your commitments, and helps you stay focused, without you having to explain yourself again each time.
