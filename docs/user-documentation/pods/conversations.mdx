---
title: "Conversations"
description: "Start conversations with agents and teammates in a Pod, let agents post to it, find past conversations, and set your notifications."
---

The **Conversations** tab lists the conversations of a Pod. All Pod members can see them and take part in them. Agents in the Pod can also search past conversations and use them as context.

## Start a conversation in a Pod

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Conversations tab">
    Open the Pod and click **Conversations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your message">
    Type your message and mention an agent with `@`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Send the message. The conversation is created in the Pod, and all members can see it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Mention people and agents

Use `@` to mention agents and teammates in a conversation:

- **Mention an agent** to make it answer in the conversation.
- **Mention a teammate** to notify them.

If you mention someone who isn't a member of the Pod yet, Dust asks you to add them. See [Members and roles](/docs/user-documentation/pods/members-and-roles).

## Let agents post to a Pod

Agents can start conversations in a Pod and post to them, for example to share a progress update or a weekly summary. They use the Pods skill, which all agents have by default.

To have an event start a conversation in a Pod automatically, use a trigger:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a trigger to an agent">
    Add a schedule or a webhook trigger to an agent. See [Triggers](/docs/user-documentation/agents/triggers/index).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the Pod in the instructions">
    In the agent's instructions, name the Pod to post to. The agent finds it by name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let the trigger run">
    When the trigger runs, the agent starts a conversation in the Pod with the details of the event.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For example, a new ticket can open a conversation in your Support Pod, or a schedule can post a daily brief every morning.

## Find conversations

In a Pod with several members, filter the list:

| Filter | Shows |
|---|---|
| **All** | All the conversations of the Pod. |
| **Mine** | The conversations where you sent a message. |
| **Group** | The conversations with more than one person. |

Click **Hide triggered** to hide the conversations started by triggers. This only changes your own list. To search the content of all the Pod's conversations, use the search bar above the list.

## Join an Open Pod

If you aren't a member of an Open Pod, the **Conversations** tab shows "Join this Pod to participate in conversations." Click **Join the [Pod name] Pod** to join.

In a Restricted Pod, you see "You need to be invited to participate in this Pod." An Editor must invite you.

## Set your notifications

Each member chooses their own notifications for each Pod:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Pod settings">
    Click the gear icon (**Pod settings**) at the top right of the Pod.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your notifications">
    In **Notifications**, choose **All activity**, **Only when mentioned**, or **Don't notify me**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You choose where you get notifications (in the app, on Slack, or by email) in your profile settings.
