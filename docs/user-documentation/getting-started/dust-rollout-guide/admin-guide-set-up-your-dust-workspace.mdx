In Dust, you own and control all your business data. We don't train models on your company data, and your usage doesn't affect how our models learn. Our platform is SOC 2 compliant, with all conversations encrypted both in transit and at rest.

With the admin console, managing team members is simple. You can also use domain verification, SSO, and access usage insights.

## Enterprise-grade security and privacy

- Customer prompts and company data are not used for training models.
- Data encryption at rest (AES 256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+)
- Certified SOC 2 compliant
- GDPR compliant

**For more information, please visit our [Trust Center](https://trust.dust.com) or contact us.**

## User management and role-based access control

Dust uses a three-role model — **Admin**, **Manager**, and **Member** — combined with governance capabilities granted through groups. This replaces the previous Admin / Builder / Member model.

- **Admin** (IT/Security): full workspace administration — connections, spaces, SSO, billing, audit logs, governance settings, API keys, model providers, developer tools, and analytics.
- **Manager** (champion/department lead): scoped operational administrator — member management, manual groups, role assignment (non-admin), analytics, and seat-type editing.
- **Member**: uses workspace resources according to seat type, space membership, and governance capabilities granted through groups.

Governance capabilities (such as `Create agents`, `Publish agents`, `Create skills`) are configured per workspace and can be restricted to specific groups or to workspace admins only.

For the complete governance model, see [Admin governance](/docs/user-documentation/admins/users-and-permissions-management/admin-governance).
