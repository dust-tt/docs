A strong builder community makes Dust adoption stick. In Dust, **builders** are the people in your organisation who create agents and skills — it is a practice and a community, not a workspace role. Their creation rights come from workspace governance capabilities and group membership.

## Granting creation rights

Two governance capabilities control who can create agents and skills:

- **`Create agents`**: controls who can create new agents (default: **Everyone** in the workspace).
- **`Create skills`**: controls who can create new skills (default: **Everyone** in the workspace).

Each capability can be set to **Everyone**, **Groups** (allowlist), or **Admin only** in **Settings & Governance**. If you want to limit creation to a dedicated group, create a group (for example **Builders**), grant it the `Create agents` and/or `Create skills` capabilities, and add your builders to it.

<Info>
**Migration note:** Workspaces that previously used the Builder role have a **Builders** group automatically created on migration, with `Create agents` and `Create skills` granted to it, and all former builders added as members.
</Info>

See [Admin governance](/docs/user-documentation/admins/users-and-permissions-management/admin-governance) for full details on capabilities, groups, and capability modes.

## Who should be a builder?

### Ideal candidates

- Employees with a deep understanding of their team workflow and know-how AND willing to learn new AI skills
- Department leads with efficiency objectives
- Anyone passionate about improving team efficiency

### Key qualities

- Problem-solving and/or tinkerer mindset
- Understanding of department workflows
- Willingness to share knowledge and help other team members

## Building your builders community

### 1. Identify builders

- Reach out directly to people you know could be interested or run a survey to identify who uses LLMs regularly
- Look across all departments or identify key teams depending on your chosen strategy
- Focus on enthusiasm and potential
- Welcome builders at all levels

### 2. Create builder hub

- Dedicated Slack channel
- Regular builder meetups
- Create a knowledge sharing and template library hub

### 3. Train and support builders

- Provide training - train the trainer
- Share best practices
- Offer technical support
- Recognize achievements
- Ask the Dust team for support on this

## Builder responsibilities

- Create team-specific agents
- Document use cases and templates
- Train team members
- Share success stories
- Gather feedback
- Share feedback with the Dust team
