---
title: "How do I change the language in Dust?"
---

Dust supports English (US), English (UK), French, and German for the product interface.

### Choose your personal language

Open **Personal Settings → Customization → Language** and select your preferred language. For the Dust interface, your personal choice takes precedence over the workspace default and applies across your workspaces.

Your personal language may not apply in every context. Shared experiences, such as workspace invitations and integrations, use the workspace default language instead.

### Set a workspace default

Open your **workspace settings**, select a language, and save it. The workspace language is used for members who have not selected a personal language and as the default for shared experiences.

Each member can use a different language by setting a personal preference.

### Don't use your browser's translation

Use the language setting in Dust rather than your browser's translation feature, such as the **Translate** option in Google Chrome. Browser translation rewrites the text on the page while Dust is running. This can conflict with how the Dust interface updates, and the page can show an error, go blank or stop responding.

Signs that browser translation is on:

- Your browser shows a translate icon in the address bar, or a translation bar at the top of the page.
- Parts of the interface are in a language you didn't choose in Dust, or the page mixes two languages.

If the interface is in English, French or German because you selected that language in Dust, that's not browser translation.

To fix it:

1. Turn off translation for Dust in your browser. In Chrome, click the translate icon in the address bar, open its menu and choose **Never translate this site**.
2. Reload the page.
3. If you want Dust in another language, choose it in **Personal Settings → Customization → Language** instead.

If the problem continues with translation turned off, contact `support@dust.tt`.
