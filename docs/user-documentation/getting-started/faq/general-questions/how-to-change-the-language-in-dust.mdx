---
title: "How do I change the language in Dust?"
---

Dust supports English (US), English (UK), and French for the product interface.

### Choose your personal language

Open **Personal Settings → Customization → Language** and select your preferred language. Your personal choice takes precedence over the workspace default and applies across your workspaces.

### Set a workspace default

Open your **workspace settings**, select a language, and save it. The workspace language is used for members who have not selected a personal language. It is also used when Dust needs a language for a shared experience, such as workspace invitations or integrations.

Each member can use a different language by setting a personal preference.
