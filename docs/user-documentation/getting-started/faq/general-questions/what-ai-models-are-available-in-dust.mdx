---
title: "What AI models are available in Dust?"
---


Dust gives you access to models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Mistral, xAI and others, plus three **auto models** that let Dust route each request for you:

- **Fast**: quick and low cost, for classification, extraction and triage.
- **Standard**: the default for the `@dust` agent and for new agents, for everyday work.
- **Complex**: slower and most capable, for hard reasoning and high-stakes work.

You choose a model in two places: the model picker in the conversation input bar, and **Advanced** > **Model selection** in the agent builder. See [Auto models and the model picker](/docs/user-documentation/agents/model-selection).

Admins can cap which models and reasoning efforts each member is allowed to select. See [Model access tiers](/docs/user-documentation/admins/usage-seats-and-credits/model-access-tiers).

The exact list of available models changes as providers ship new ones. The picker always shows what is currently available to you.

- For security-related questions, check: [https://dust.tt/home/security](https://dust.tt/home/security)
- To better understand what models to use, check [Create your first agent](/docs/user-documentation/agents/create-your-first-agent)
