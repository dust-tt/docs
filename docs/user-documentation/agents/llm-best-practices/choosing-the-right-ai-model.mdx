Each model family has its own strengths, for example for writing, reasoning, or coding. Our blog article compares the main model families to help you choose the model of your agent.

[Read the full article on the Dust blog](https://dust.tt/blog/comparing-ai-models-claude-gpt4-gemini-mistral)

To let Dust pick the model for you, use an auto model. See [Auto models and the model picker](/docs/user-documentation/agents/model-selection).
