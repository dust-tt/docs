---
title: "Discover Knowledge"
description: "Let your agent search, browse, and read your company data, and query your data warehouses with SQL."
---

Discover Knowledge lets an agent find information in your company data. It searches and reads documents from your connected sources, and queries your data warehouses with SQL. **@dust** already has it, and you can add it to your own agents.

## What your agent can do with Discover Knowledge

| Capability | What it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| Search company data | Finds relevant content across your connected sources, even when a document uses different words than your question. |
| Browse sources | Explores folders and finds documents, pages, and tables by name. |
| Read documents | Reads a document or page it found, and sees where it sits in the source. |
| Explore data warehouses | Lists databases, schemas, and tables, and looks at columns and sample values. |
| Query data warehouses | Runs SQL on your warehouse tables to count, rank, compare, or find trends. |

For a question about numbers, the agent can also read your documents first, like a metric's definition, before it queries the warehouse.

## When to use Discover Knowledge

Add Discover Knowledge when your agent needs to:

- Answer questions from documents spread across several connected sources.
- Find a document when you don't know its title or where it is.
- Look through a whole folder or collection.
- Answer questions with numbers from your data warehouse, and explain them with context from your documents.

## Add Discover Knowledge to an agent

**@dust** already has Discover Knowledge.

To add Discover Knowledge to your own agent:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your agent in the Agent Builder">
    Create an agent, or open one you can edit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the skill">
    In **Capabilities and knowledge**, click **Add capabilities** and select **Discover Knowledge**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your agent">
    The agent can now search your company data when a question needs it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Example instructions

```text
When a question is about our internal processes, search company data with Discover Knowledge first, and link to the documents you used.
```

## Limits

- Discover Knowledge searches the data in **Company Data** and in the other Spaces the agent uses.
- It only reads data. To create or update records in other apps, use [Discover Tools](/docs/user-documentation/agents/discover-tools) or add the tool to your agent.
