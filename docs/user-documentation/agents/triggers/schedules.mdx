---
title: "Schedules"
description: "Run an agent at set times, like every weekday morning or the first day of each month, by describing the schedule in plain language."
---

A **schedule** runs an agent automatically at the times you choose, for example a daily digest, a weekly report, or a monthly review. You describe when it should run in plain language, and Dust turns it into a schedule.

## Add a schedule to an agent

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the agent's triggers">
    Open the agent in the **Agent Builder**. In the **Triggers** section, click **Add triggers**, then pick **Schedule**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe when it runs">
    Give the trigger a **Name**. In **Scheduler**, describe the timing in plain language, like _"every weekday at 8 AM"_ or _"the first Monday of each month at 9 AM"_. The schedule uses your timezone unless you name another one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the schedule">
    Dust shows how it understood your description, for example _"At 8:00 AM, Monday through Friday, in Los Angeles (America/Los_Angeles) timezone."_ Expand **Next 5 occurrences** to check the upcoming runs. If it isn't right, rephrase your description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a message">
    In **Message (optional)**, tell the agent what to do on each run, like _"Summarize yesterday's new support tickets."_
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose credits and where runs appear">
    Pick whose credits pay for the runs under **Credits**. See [Credits usage](/docs/user-documentation/agents/triggers/credits-usage). To share the runs with your team, pick a Pod under **Where to create this conversation? (optional)**. Otherwise, runs appear in your own conversations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the agent">
    The schedule starts once you save the agent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How often a schedule can run

A schedule can run at most once per hour. Schedules such as every hour, every weekday at 8 AM, or the first day of each month work. Anything more frequent, such as every 15 or 30 minutes, is refused with the error "Unable to generate a schedule: it can't be more frequent than hourly. Please try rephrasing."

The same limit applies when an agent creates a schedule for you from a conversation.

## Who can add a trigger

You can add a trigger to an agent only if you can use that agent:

- The agent is published, or you're one of its editors.
- You have access to every Space that the agent's tools, knowledge, and skills come from.

Otherwise, saving the trigger fails with the error "The agent configuration was not found." Ask one of the agent's editors or an admin which Spaces the agent relies on, and request access to them.
